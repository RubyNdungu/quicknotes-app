About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a fast and easy way to capture your thoughts on the go. Use it to capture your great ideas.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought into the input box on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  3. Review all your saved notes in the list below.
